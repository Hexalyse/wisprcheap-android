# WisprCheap Android 0.2.7

- **History privacy & safe audio paths.** Keep session-only entries recorded with "Keep a history" disabled out of disk snapshots and sync, restrict failed-audio reads/deletes to canonical files inside the local recordings directory, and only show Retry for owned local recordings.
- **Sync clock-skew recovery & lifecycle safety.** Recover from skewed device clocks using `serverTime` without poisoning the local HLC or dropping `clock_skew`-rejected history entries, strip `audioFile` paths on upload and download, and serialize sync runs/disconnects so an in-flight sync cannot resurrect removed state.
- **Accessibility text insertion fixes.** Record the method that actually succeeded when learning per-app insertion strategies, and honor configured/learned methods while verifying `commitText` during selection replacement.

Default encryption parameters and sync interoperability remain compatible.
